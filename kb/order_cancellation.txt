An order can be cancelled directly from the Nykaa application or website while it remains in the Placed or Processing stage. Once an order has been assigned a courier tracking number and moved to Shipped status, direct one-click cancellation is disabled. If an order is already shipped, customers should refuse delivery when the courier partner arrives at their doorstep. Prepaid cancellations trigger an immediate reversal to the original payment source within 24 to 48 hours.
