If money is debited from your bank account or UPI application but the Nykaa order screen indicates payment failure, the transaction is marked pending. Banking gateways automatically reconcile pending transactions and initiate a full reversal within 24 to 48 banking hours. If your funds are not credited back within 3 business days, provide the 12-digit UPI reference number or bank UTR to customer support. Customers are advised not to place duplicate orders until receiving a definitive SMS or email status confirmation from Nykaa.
