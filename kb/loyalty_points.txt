Nykaa Reward Points are accrued at a rate of 1 point for every 100 rupees spent on completed purchases across all categories. Every accumulated point holds a redemption value of 1 rupee and can be applied at the checkout screen against future orders. Reward points remain active for 365 calendar days from their date of accrual before expiring automatically. Points earned on returned or refunded orders are deducted from the customer loyalty wallet balance upon return approval.
