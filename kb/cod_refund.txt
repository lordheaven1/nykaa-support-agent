For orders placed using Cash on Delivery (COD), refunds are processed directly into the customer registered bank account via NEFT or IMPS. Customers must securely enter and verify their account number and IFSC code under the Payment Settings tab in the Nykaa app. Once our fulfillment warehouse inspects and approves the returned item, COD refunds are credited within 5 to 7 business days. Nykaa does not disburse refunds in physical cash or paper cheques.
