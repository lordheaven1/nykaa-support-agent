Level 1 customer support is managed by our chat and email agents, who address general inquiries and order updates within 24 hours. If a grievance remains unresolved or a shipment delay exceeds 48 hours, the ticket escalates to a Level 2 Team Lead. Delays surpassing 5 days or claims involving disputed refunds and missing packages are routed directly to Level 3 Senior Operations. Unresolved matters may be submitted to the Grievance Officer, who provides a formal written resolution within 15 business days in compliance with statutory e-commerce guidelines.
