Nykaa ships select Beauty and Wellness catalog items internationally to over 30 countries across North America, Europe, and Asia. International shipments require 7 to 14 business days for customs clearance and final doorstep handover. Any destination import duties, clearance tariffs, or local value-added taxes are the sole responsibility of the recipient. Perishable items, aerosol cans, nail polishes, and heavy electronics are strictly prohibited from international export due to civil aviation safety regulations.
