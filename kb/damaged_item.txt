If an item arrives damaged, leaking, broken, or tampered with, customers must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery. Customers must upload clear photographs of the outer shipping box, the damaged item, and the visible courier shipping label through the support portal. Our internal verification team audits the photographic proof and responds with an approval decision within 24 hours. Upon validation, Nykaa initiates a free replacement or processes a complete refund including any applicable shipping charges.
