Nykaa provides complimentary reverse pickup services across more than 18,000 serviceable Indian pincodes. Once a return request is authorized, our courier partner attempts pickup from the delivery address within 24 to 48 hours. The customer must keep the product unused with all original tags, boxes, and barcode stickers securely packed. If a pincode is non-serviceable for reverse pickup, the customer must self-ship the item, and Nykaa reimburses up to 100 rupees in courier costs upon receipt of a valid postal receipt.
