Nykaa allows return requests within 15 days of delivery for Apparel and Footwear products. Beauty, makeup, cosmetics, skincare, and personal care items carry a strict 5-day return window and must remain unopened with their original seal intact. Electronics and styling appliances must be returned within 7 days in their original packaging with all included accessories. Items marked as final clearance or free promotional gifts are strictly non-returnable under any circumstance.
