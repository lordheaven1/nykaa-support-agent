Electronic appliances and personal styling tools sold on Nykaa carry a standard 1-year or 2-year manufacturer brand warranty. Nykaa issues a GST tax invoice that serves as official proof of purchase for all warranty claims at authorized brand service centers. Physical wear and tear, liquid immersion, or accidental dropping are excluded from manufacturer warranty coverage. For initial out-of-box defects identified within 7 days of delivery, Nykaa handles the replacement directly before referring the customer to the brand.
